About Puzzles

Every good relationship theory starts the same way: a whiteboard, a marker, and a friend who's a little too confident about plotting people on a chart. Ours started with a graph — two axes, a handful of zones, and one suspiciously specific corner labeled the Zone of Pain. We didn't invent the idea. We just gave it real math, a database, and slightly too much free time.

Why Is It Called Puzzles?

That's the puzzle!

(We'll see ourselves out.)

Is It Any Good?

Look, we're not going to tell you it's legen — wait for it — dary. That would be excessive. But it's pretty good. It's genuinely, believably, defensibly pretty good.

What We Actually Believe

People aren't spreadsheets. But your patterns kind of are — and patterns are exactly the sort of thing you can't see while you're standing inside them. That's the whole point here: not to reduce anyone to a number, but to catch the thing you keep doing without noticing you're doing it.

The Legal-Sounding Part

Your data stays on your device. We're not a dating app, a matchmaking service, or a replacement for an actual conversation with an actual person you trust. We're a mirror with a sense of humor. Use responsibly. Text back.

More
🤞 Your Privacy

Pinky promise: your data stays right where you put it — in your own browser, on your own device. No servers quietly watching, no "trusted partners," no fine print doing something sneaky. The only exception is the one button you choose to press yourself, which kicks off a brief, consensual chat about your life choices — and nothing else, ever, without you asking for it.

How Puzzles Works

Four steps, in order. Nothing else to figure out.

1
🧩 PLOT
Enter their name and gender, rate them, set your Status and Intent, then hit Analyse & Log. This saves them.
2
📋 LOG
See everyone you've logged. Once you've got at least 2 people in there, hit Copy Prompt & Open Claude — it pastes your data into a new Claude chat for you, where you'll get an honest read on your patterns.
3
🗺️ MAP
See where everyone lands on the chart. Optional: you can even edit where the zone boundaries sit.
